1

Stays on his computer

  • Speech-to-text and the AI model run locally
  • His voice is never saved, not even on his computer
  • A privacy filter strips names, phones and addresses before the AI sees the text
2

What leaves

  • Coded fields only: symptom, severity, onset, check-in status
  • No audio, no words, no name
  • Nothing is sent until Walter approves it
  • He keeps a receipt with a fingerprint of exactly what was sent
3

Who can read it

  • Doctor: sealed to his care team's key. Our server can't open it
  • Company: groups of 5 or more, by week, never a name
  • Caregiver: alerts only, never symptoms
  • Emergency hospital: only with a stated reason, logged

Who pays

  • The drug company: it has a legal duty to monitor side effects
  • $100k per drug program a year + $25 per patient a month
  • 1,000 patients = $400k a program, every year
  • Patients and doctors use it free. Data is never sold.

Privacy is the margin

  • The AI runs on the patient's device: no cloud AI bill per patient
  • We store small coded records, not audio or transcripts
  • The company holds less identifiable data, so less risk
  • New drug, same app: only the survey changes
